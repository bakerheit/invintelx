## Why this exists

InvIntelX is licensed under the GNU Affero General Public Licence v3.0. That
licence governs what *you* may do with the code.

This agreement governs something different: what the project may do with *your*
contribution. Without it, every outside contribution is licensed to the project
under AGPL and nothing else, which means the project can never offer a
commercial licence to someone who needs one, and can never change licence
without tracking down every contributor who ever sent a patch.

Signing does **not** transfer ownership. You keep the copyright in your
contribution and remain free to use it however you like, including in other
projects under other licences.

## Contributions on behalf of an employer

If your employer has rights in work you produce — which is common, and often
true even for work done on your own time and equipment — then you need your
employer's permission before signing, or a signature from someone authorised to
bind them. If you are unsure, ask before contributing rather than after.

## How to sign

Open a pull request adding your name to `CONTRIBUTORS.md` in this format, and
say in the pull request body that you have read and agree to this agreement:

```
- Full Name <email@example.com> (@github-handle) — signed YYYY-MM-DD
```

One signature covers all your contributions, past and future. You do not need
to sign again for each pull request.
